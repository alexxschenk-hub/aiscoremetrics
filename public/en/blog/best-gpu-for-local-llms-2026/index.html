Best GPU for Local LLMs in 2026 – Buying Guide

For local language models, exactly two things matter on a graphics card: how much VRAM it has (does the model fit at all?) and how much memory bandwidth it offers (how fast does it write?). Compute comes second – it mostly decides how long you wait for the first word. This guide sorts current cards by budget and compares the three most popular 24 GB cards head to head.

🎯What matters when buying

  • VRAM decides which models run. 16 GB is enough for models up to about 14 billion parameters, 24 GB for 27B models and the popular 30B MoE models, 32 GB leaves room for longer context. How to work out your needs is covered in How much VRAM do I need?
  • Bandwidth decides speed. For every generated token the GPU reads the active weights once from memory. Twice the bandwidth means roughly twice the tokens per second.
  • Compute decides the wait. Long prompts – a whole document or a code repository – are processed with tensor throughput. NVIDIA cards lead clearly here.
  • Software: NVIDIA (CUDA) works with every tool out of the box. AMD runs well under ROCm or Vulkan with llama.cpp, LM Studio and Ollama; image and training tools need more tinkering.

Entry level up to €750

The RTX 5060 Ti 16 GB is the cheapest sensible CUDA entry. Its weak spot is the low 448 GB/s bandwidth. The RX 9070 XT costs a little more but writes about 20% faster. Both have 16 GB: gpt-oss-20b just fits with short context, at 32k it gets too tight.

Mid-range around €1,100 to €1,500

The RTX 5070 Ti has the same 16 GB but twice the bandwidth of the 5060 Ti – small models run twice as fast. If you want bigger models, go for 24 GB instead: the RX 7900 XTX is currently the cheapest new card with 24 GB.

High end

The RTX 4090 is being phased out: new, at around €5,000, it costs almost as much as an RTX 5090. The RTX 5090 is the fastest consumer card at 1,792 GB/s and the only one with 32 GB – at a price where a Mac Studio becomes an option too. That comparison is in RTX 5090 vs Mac Studio M5 Ultra.

For writing, all three are close. The 3090 reaches over 90% of the 4090's speed because the bandwidth is almost the same. With long prompts the 4090 pulls ahead: it reads a 16k-token document in about 5 seconds, the 3090 needs about 12, the 7900 XTX about 20 seconds. For image generation the 4090 is more than twice as fast.

🧪Which card for whom?

  • RTX 3090 (used): the best value for text-only AI if you find a good card. Check warranty, fans and memory temperatures.
  • RX 7900 XTX: 24 GB new with warranty for about a third of the 4090's price. Ideal for chat and coding with llama.cpp or LM Studio. Less suited if you do a lot of images, video or training.
  • RTX 4090: only worth it if you get it cheap (used, for example). For agents with long prompts and for image generation it's noticeably better than the other two.

✅Verdict

Under €1,000: RX 9070 XT or RTX 5060 Ti 16 GB. Best value with 24 GB: a used RTX 3090 or a new RX 7900 XTX. No compromises: RTX 5090 – or, if models above 30 GB should run, a Mac Studio with lots of unified memory.
