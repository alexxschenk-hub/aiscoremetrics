Around €7,000 buys you either a PC with an RTX 5090 or a Mac Studio with M5 Ultra and 96 GB. Both are the fastest devices in their class, but they are built for completely different models: the 5090 for speed, the Mac for size.

On the Mac, CPU and GPU share memory. macOS gives the GPU about three quarters of it by default; one terminal command raises the limit. The calculator has a “GPU memory limit raised” switch for this.

Models up to 32 GB: the 5090 is almost twice as fast

As long as the model fits in the 5090's 32 GB, it wins clearly – thanks to 50% more bandwidth and much more compute. Values with Q4_K_M and 32k context:

Large models: only the Mac wins here

From about 30 GB of memory demand, the 5090 is out. The model would have to spill into regular PC memory, which at roughly 60 to 90 GB/s has a fraction of the bandwidth – speed then collapses.

Large MoE models like gpt-oss-120b run surprisingly fast on the Mac because only a few billion parameters are active per token. With 256 GB (€12,429) even Qwen3 235B-A22B fits at about 20 tok/s.

Images and video

For image generation the 5090 leads, but the gap is smaller than many assume: FLUX.1 dev takes an estimated 11 seconds per 1024 × 1024 image on the 5090 and about 16 seconds on the M5 Ultra with 64 GPU cores (13 seconds with 80 cores). The M5's Neural Accelerators close a lot of the gap. A 5-second 720p clip with Wan 2.2 A14B takes about 2 minutes on the 5090 and about 3 minutes on the M5 Ultra, according to the calculator. For video workflows and tools like ComfyUI, the CUDA ecosystem is still more mature.

Power, noise, everyday use

A 5090 PC draws about 725 W under AI load, the Mac Studio about 190 W. At 8 hours of load per day and €0.25 per kWh that's roughly €530 versus €140 in electricity per year. On top of that, the Mac stays quiet under full load, while the 5090 needs a good case and a strong power supply.

Verdict: which is worth it?

RTX 5090 if you work with models up to about 30B, care about short waits (coding agents), generate images or video, or train yourself. Mac Studio M5 Ultra if you want to run the big open models (70B, gpt-oss-120b, GLM-4.5-Air) locally – no single consumer graphics card can do that.

If you only need large models occasionally, also look at the DGX Spark or a Mac Studio with M5 Max.
